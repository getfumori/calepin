# Calepin Brand Direction

## Name and meaning

**Calepin** is the accepted product name. It replaced the earlier working
name Fumori.

`Calepin` is a French word for a small pocket notebook. It comes from
Ambrogio Calepino, an Italian lexicographer whose dictionary, first printed
in 1502, became so common that his name came to mean a book you keep at hand
for reference and notes.

The name fits the product: one notebook that the owner keeps close for notes,
quick capture, and tasks.

## Product metaphor

- Quick captures and Daily Notes are entries jotted into the notebook.
- Markdown notes are pages worth keeping.
- Tasks are the notebook's to-do margin, checked off and carried forward.
- AI may read the notebook but never silently rewrites what the owner wrote.

## Character and visual direction

The brand should feel quiet, literary, private, practical, and long-lived.

The current Web client uses deep green-black on warm ivory with an editorial
serif. A logo that fits the Calepin name has not been designed yet.

## Logo status

The image under [`docs/brand/`](../brand/) is a generated raster concept made
for the earlier Fumori name. It is kept only as a record of the color and
typography direction. It is not a Calepin logo and must not ship.

A future brand-production task should design an original SVG mark for
Calepin, then derive horizontal, symbol-only app icon, monochrome, and
dark-mode variants.

## Name clearance

Preliminary screening, done before the rename:

- The iOS and Mac App Stores have no app named Calepin. The closest name is
  LeCalepin, a French local-services marketplace.
- GitHub has several small note projects named calepin, none of them
  established. The closest is a personal Markdown notes and tasks app with no
  stars.
- `calepin.com`, `calepin.app`, and `calepin.dev` are registered by others.
- Calepin is an ordinary French noun, so it is hard to rank for in French
  search and may be weak as a trademark there.

This screening is not formal trademark or legal clearance. Domains, package
names, social handles, and legal availability must be checked again
immediately before registration or public launch.
